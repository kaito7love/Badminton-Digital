Frequently Asked Questions

What type of footwear is required on the courts?
Only non-marking indoor court shoes are allowed on all synthetic BWF court mats to preserve floor grip and prevent surface scuffing.
What is your booking cancellation & refund policy?
You can cancel or reschedule any court reservation up to 6 hours before your slot start time for a full account credit or refund.
Can I rent rackets and buy shuttlecocks on arrival?
Yes! Our Pro Shop stocks high-end Yonex and Victor rental rackets, feather shuttle tubes, grips, and restringing services.
